### Two-phase contact boundary

Administrative approval contact cannot require the resulting approval receipt
in advance. The protocol therefore separates:

1. **administrative-contact phase:** populated instance, independent review,
   and fresh scoped administrative-contact authority must exist before a
   durable intent record and the first license/governance/source contact;
2. **data-access phase:** contact outcomes and all required approvals must be
   recorded, then a separately reviewed access instance and fresh scoped data-
   access authority must exist before any data access or download.

Every future contact or attempted access must first reserve a durable
no-clobber intent record by `O_EXCL`, fsync the file and parent directory, and
then append exactly one hash-linked success, denial, failure, or cancellation
outcome. Each roster row follows
`PLANNED -> INTENT_CLAIMED -> OUTCOME_RECORDED`; an absent outcome after a
durable intent remains a terminal spent/incomplete operation and is not a
retry permission. A phase advances only after every declared row in that phase
has a terminal outcome. Credentials, tokens, cookies, and raw secrets are
forbidden from the log.

The state machine is forward-only:

1. `DESIGN_FROZEN_AWAITING_POPULATED_PRECONTACT_INSTANCE`;
2. `PRECONTACT_INSTANCE_POPULATED_AWAITING_INDEPENDENT_REVIEW`;
3. `PRECONTACT_INSTANCE_REVIEWED_AWAITING_FRESH_ADMIN_CONTACT_AUTHORITY`;
4. `ADMIN_CONTACT_AUTHORIZED_AWAITING_DURABLE_INTENT`;
5. `ADMIN_CONTACT_INTENT_RESERVED_AWAITING_CONTACT`;
6. `ADMIN_CONTACT_OUTCOME_RECORDED_AWAITING_REQUIRED_APPROVALS`;
7. `APPROVALS_COMPLETE_AWAITING_REVIEWED_DATA_ACCESS_INSTANCE`;
8. `DATA_ACCESS_INSTANCE_REVIEWED_AWAITING_FRESH_DATA_ACCESS_AUTHORITY`;
9. `DATA_ACCESS_AUTHORIZED_AWAITING_DURABLE_INTENT`;
10. `DATA_ACCESS_INTENT_RESERVED_AWAITING_ACCESS`;
11. `SNAPSHOT_OBSERVED_AND_CUSTODIED_AWAITING_DETERMINISTIC_SPLIT`;
12. `SPLIT_ASSIGNED_AND_HELD_OUT_ESCROW_ACTIVE`;
13. `FINAL_OPENING_AWAITING_SEPARATE_AUTHORITY`.

Only an exact successful outcome satisfying the row's frozen success predicate
may advance. `DENIED`, `FAILED`, or `CANCELLED` administrative outcomes,
incomplete required approvals, an unavailable selected version, acquisition-
selector mismatch, snapshot identity/hash mismatch, or a failed data-access
operation enters the corresponding terminal no-go state:
`ADMIN_CONTACT_TERMINAL_NO_GO`, `APPROVALS_INCOMPLETE_TERMINAL_NO_GO`,
`SELECTOR_OR_SNAPSHOT_TERMINAL_NO_GO`, or
`DATA_ACCESS_TERMINAL_NO_GO`. No terminal no-go state permits a replacement
source, selector change, new operation, or retry.

The exact frozen failure map is total over the named failure classes:

- `ADMIN_DENIED`, `ADMIN_FAILED`, and `ADMIN_CANCELLED` map to
  `ADMIN_CONTACT_TERMINAL_NO_GO`;
- `REQUIRED_APPROVALS_INCOMPLETE` maps to
  `APPROVALS_INCOMPLETE_TERMINAL_NO_GO`;
- `SELECTED_VERSION_UNAVAILABLE`, `ACQUISITION_SELECTOR_MISMATCH`, and
  `SNAPSHOT_IDENTITY_OR_HASH_MISMATCH` map to
  `SELECTOR_OR_SNAPSHOT_TERMINAL_NO_GO`; and
- `DATA_ACCESS_DENIED`, `DATA_ACCESS_FAILED`, and `DATA_ACCESS_CANCELLED` map
  to `DATA_ACCESS_TERMINAL_NO_GO`.

No omitted or unknown outcome is success. An intent with no observed outcome
uses `TERMINAL_SPENT_INCOMPLETE_NO_RETRY`; a populated row must name its own
success predicate and terminal disposition before review can admit it.

Contact before its required populated instance, review, fresh authority, and
durable intent is a terminal protocol violation. Data access before completed
approval receipts and the separately authorized access phase is also a
terminal violation. Deletion, resplitting, or reacquisition cannot erase the
violation.

No instance, reviewer receipt, contact authority record, intent record,
