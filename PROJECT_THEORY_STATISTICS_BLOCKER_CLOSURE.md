### 3.3 Confidence and Holm decision

For each domain, the F137 aggregation produces one complete paired statistic
`M[d,s]` in `[-3,3]` for each of the 256 frozen training seeds. Conditional on
the separately required independent seed-stream law, Hoeffding gives the
one-sided lower confidence bound

```text
L_d(alpha) = mean_s M[d,s]
             - 6 sqrt(log(1/alpha)/(2*256)).
```

The code encloses logarithms and square roots upward with exact rational
arithmetic, so the returned rational lower endpoint is conservative. The
equivalent one-sided p-value is bounded by

```text
p_d = exp(-2*256*max(mean_s M[d,s] - 1/100, 0)^2/36).
```

No exponential evaluation is needed for the decision. Domains are ordered by
the exact exponent, largest first; an exact tie orders R3-PHYS before
R4-RETAIL. The first rejects only when its exponent is at least a certified
upper enclosure of `log(40)`. The second rejects only after the first and when
its exponent is at least a certified upper enclosure of `log(20)`. This is a
conservative exact-arithmetic implementation of the frozen two-domain Holm
rule. Both domains must reject; one cannot rescue the other.

confirmatory coverage. There is no bootstrap fallback. The separate F105
