## 7. Definition of “done”

A scientific-closure checkbox may be changed to `[x]` only when:

1. the exact preregistration field, blocker, Test-28 production blocker, or
   Formal Test 28/29/30 predicate is named;
2. a durable evidence path or receipt exists;
3. the responsible owner has reviewed it;
4. any required independent review has completed;
5. the master scorecard count is updated; and
6. no later result was used to make the decision.

Drafts, schemas, hashes, test counts, meetings, and newly added layers are
activities. They are not completion unless they close a named item.

A **project-control** checkbox may instead be checked without reducing the
scientific scorecard only when it names an exact pre-outcome control predicate,
has durable content-addressed evidence, has responsible-owner and independent
review, states the unchanged field/blocker/test/result counts, and used no later
result. Such a checkmark must be labelled project-control and does not satisfy a
preregistration field, blocker, Formal Test, admission, authorization, or result.
