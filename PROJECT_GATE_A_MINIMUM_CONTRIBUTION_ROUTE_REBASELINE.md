## 3. Exact contribution-state algebra

Each required component has exactly one of three states:

- `PENDING`: the required frozen evidence is not yet complete;
- `PASS`: the exact required evidence and custody checks have passed; or
- `TERMINAL_NO_GO`: the component has a terminal failure, invalidity,
  nonadmission, or nonreproducibility outcome.

The exact project-control predicates are:

```text
EMPIRICAL_CONTRIBUTION_GO
  := every required component is PASS.

EMPIRICAL_CONTRIBUTION_TERMINAL_NO_GO
  := at least one required component is TERMINAL_NO_GO.

EMPIRICAL_CONTRIBUTION_PENDING
  := neither of the predicates above is true.
```

`TERMINAL_NO_GO` is absorbing. A later result, alternate metric, changed sign,
new threshold, new seed, replacement baseline, replacement domain, different
claim family, C17 revival, or selective omission cannot reopen it. A previously
accepted component may still fail closed if a later audit invalidates its
custody or semantics, but no terminal component may return to `PENDING` or
`PASS`.

The companion validator exposes only pure state-algebra helpers. Caller-
supplied labels are not treated as evidence, so even an all-`PASS` synthetic
projection is not a scientific result and does not register the real project
predicate as achieved.

outcomes. Once any protected development or confirmatory outcome is observed,
the project may report the frozen route faithfully, but it may not switch to:

- a C17 headline or any other theory headline;
- a novel-mechanism or novel-method claim;
- a one-domain paper after the other domain fails;
- an alternate primary metric, direction, transformation, effect margin,
  confidence rule, multiplicity family, baseline, control, scaling grid,
  failure denominator, seed schedule, or domain;
- an association story without the frozen association-destroyed/factorized
  contrast; or
- a secondary-only, best-seed, successful-run-only, or clean-room-optional
  contribution criterion.

A scientifically useful negative or failure report can still be written, but
it is not `EMPIRICAL_CONTRIBUTION_GO` and does not authorize silent claim
promotion. A genuinely different future project would require new authority,
a new prospective seal and preregistration, and a clearly separated dataset
and outcome boundary; it is not a fallback within this route.
