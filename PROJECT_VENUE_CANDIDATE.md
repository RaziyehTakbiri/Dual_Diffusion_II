## Evidence and rationale

1. The current solo timetable targets a submission-ready package on 2028-03-12,
   with bounded contingency through 2028-08-27.
2. ICLR 2027 requires an abstract by 2026-09-18 and the full paper by
   2026-09-25. Those dates precede completion of Gate A and are incompatible
   with the evidence-gated plan.
3. TMLR accepts rolling submissions, uses double-blind review, provides an
   official mandatory LaTeX template, permits variable manuscript length, and
   explicitly welcomes methodological, theoretical, application, and
   reproducibility work.
4. The present manuscript is long, theory-heavy, and audit/reproducibility
   oriented. TMLR therefore fits the minimum defensible route better than a
   deadline-driven conference chosen before results exist.
5. The user-accepted NeurIPS 2027 reserve is retained as historical provenance
   but is no longer feasible under the solo timetable. A later NeurIPS
   main-track cycle is only an agent-selected reserve recommendation. No later
   year, deadline, or template is frozen until an official call exists, the
   post-G4 timing rule and scientific scope both fit, and the user accepts it.
