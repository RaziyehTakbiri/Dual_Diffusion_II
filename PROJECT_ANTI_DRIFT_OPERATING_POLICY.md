## Plain-language rules

The rules below are a summary. Codex adopts every exact Section-8 rule in the
timetable by incorporation, including the two-artifact cap and the prohibition on
another environment rehearsal absent a proven new live defect.

1. Count progress only when a named field, blocker, or gate is closed by durable evidence.
2. Do not add layers or documents merely to appear busy; new work must close a named item.
3. Do not change hypotheses, metrics, thresholds, seeds, domains, or compute after seeing outcomes.
4. Do not retry a spent one-shot attempt, add favorable seeds, top up compute, or hide failures.
5. Do not open test data or launch scientific work before the applicable frozen gate and fresh authority.
6. Preserve failed, aborted, and negative outcomes in the decision record.
7. If two consecutive weekly reviews reduce no tracked count, stop new implementation and review scope.
8. If a gate slips, move the date; never steal time from audit, reproduction, anonymity, or readiness work.
9. Keep the manuscript, timetable, evidence ledger, and scorecard synchronized.
10. Internal subagents may audit work but may not self-authorize external or scientific actions.

audit, and any separately required user or institutional authority. No change may
retroactively legitimize an action or result.
